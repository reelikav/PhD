How to read the map

Task 3 part (green): from the centre, follow produces knowledge through down to the Ways of knowing frame. Read each green arrow as a sentence: box name + the verb in the box + the trend area, e.g. Technical HCI Research builds tools for Generative AI. Grey lines show the research paradigm each way of knowing usually rests on.

Start at the centre. The five shaded rays lead to the main areas. From there, follow the arrows. Each arrow and its verb make a sentence, read in the direction of the arrow. For example, Over-reliance on AI reduces Learner agency & control.

The verb says how the two concepts are linked. Its colour shows the verb type: Scope says what a concept covers, Uses & needs what it uses or needs, and Effects what it affects.

A word in front of the verb shows how strong the claim is. With no word, the sentence is true in general. can means it is true in some cases, as in Generative AI & LLMs can support Personalized & adaptive learning. should means it is a goal rather than a finding, as in Responsible & ethical AI should protect Academic integrity.

Linking phrases four verb types: three from Task 2, Studies added for Task 3

Each verb belongs to one verb type, shown by its colour. can and should are not verb types: they can go in front of any verb to show how strong the claim is, and they do not change its type. "support" and "supports" are the same verb. focuses on appears only on the shaded rays from the centre.
